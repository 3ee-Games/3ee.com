Harmony Estates: HOA Simulator puts you in charge of the neighborhood and gives you the power to become its greatest president or its worst nightmare.

Set the rules, collect dues, issue citations, manage neighborhood amenities, and deal with residents whose personalities, finances, and choices shape how they respond to you. Build your popularity and win reelection or embrace corruption, manipulate the system, commit fraud, and find yourself facing lawsuits, jail, and a criminal life of your own making.

With a mix of simulation, RPG progression, exploration, and dark humor, every decision has consequences. Be the HOA president you would want to live under or become the one everyone loves to hate.
